Conclusion
From the brief overview of the key features
of the groin vaults designed by Baldomar, it
is obvious that he uses the trace of the arch,
skilfully mastered in all its forms in Valencia 12
at that time, as a general strategy. As such,
this approach does not focus on any one
vault, but rather on a variety of cases, the
groined vault being just like any other.
But the other goal of the article was to
discuss which approach is needed when it
comes to carrying out a stereotomic analysis
of these architectural works, trying to
overcome any prejudices we might have due

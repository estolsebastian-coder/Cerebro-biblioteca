these vaults, we have used cross-image
photogrammetry. The photographs have
been processed by PhotoModeler Scanner,
which has reported a maximum error of 0.008
meters. The obtained point cloud has been
later treated with Autocad and Rhinoceros
to elaborate a rigorous, not ideal, graphical
representation of the geometry of the vaults.
We have taken 16 photographs, obtaining
712 points which represent the surfaces
of the analyzed pieces. The vaults of the
five central passages are formed by a grid
of 10 longitudinal ribs and 5 transverse

or “en torre cavada” which corresponds to

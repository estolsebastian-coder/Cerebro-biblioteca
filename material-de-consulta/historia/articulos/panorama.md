This is a 100% recyclable toothbrush

The removable head can be replaced as and
when the user considers it necessary.

This represents major savings on materials
The heads are sold in packs of five; when all five
heads have been used, the pack can be sent
back to the production plant, where it is
recycled, The company then sends the
customer another pack, cash on delivery.
The long life of this toothbrush represents an
estimated 80% reduction in environmental
impact as compared to a normal brush

A small-scale pilot operation could have
enormous repercussions, depending on the

volume of production and extent of use

ATAUD DE CARTON RECICLADO RECYCLED CARDBOARD COFFIN

The material used to manufacture this coffin is
chlorine-free recycled cellulose, The mesh of
fibres and the cardboard structure are treated
to obtain a density which can bear a weight of
200 kg. The panels are 8 mm thick, and the

coffin waighs 12 kg. The glue used to seal

the layers is obtained from potato extract, and
a water-based solvent-free paint is used for the
surface. The incineration of a conventional solid
wooden coffin produces carbon-monoxide
emissions of 76 g, which this alternative

reduces to just 22 g.

Croquis Editorial 2004

Revista de arquitectura ampliada y revisada de
los números 20 + 64 + 98 sobre Rafael Moneo.

La editorial El Croquis publica el primer libro que
recoge de modo completo la obra significativa del
arquitecto español Rafael Moneo. El libro incluye
las obras más representativas realizadas a lo
largo de su carrera profesional, desde sus
comienzos en 1967 hasta los últimos edificios
construidos en 2004, como: el Bankinter, el
Ayuntamiento de Logroño, el Museo de Arte
Romano de Mérida, la Fundación Pilar y Joan
Miró, la Catedral de Nuestra Señora de Los
Ángeles, el Auditorio de Música de Barcelona, el Hospital Materno Infantil
Gregorio Marañón o la Ampliación del Museo del Prado. Todas las obras se
presentan con profusión de planos, dibujos y fotografías para procurar una cabal
comprensión por parte del lector interesado. La documentación gráfica se
acompaña de tres entrevistas al autor, varios artículos críticos y seis ensayos
teóricos y críticos.

Houston
by Martha Thorne (text), Joe C. Aker, Gary Zvonkovic (Photographers) Edition

The Museum of Fine Arts, Houston, is a
unique collection of architectural works —

<!-- p. 2 -->

the Caroline Wiess Law Building, comprising
the original William Ward Watkins Building of
1924 and the 1958 and 1974 additions
designed by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe: The
Lillie and Hugh Roy Cullen Sculpture Garden
created by Isamu Noguchi in 1986; the
Central Administration and Glassell Junior
School Building designed by Carlos Jiménez
in 1994; and now the Audrey Jones Beck
Building by Rafael Moneo.

Moneo has proposed a four-story facility
directly facing the Law Building and
connected to it via an underground walkway.
The limestone building occupies the whole
site, thereby reinforcing its urban character.
On the inside, visitors can assemble in the
dramatic atrium before proceeding to the
upper level galleries to begin their itinerary.

The Beck Building is a natural progression of some of the ideas put forth by the
architect in previous museum projects, especially the Museo ThyssenBornemisza in Madrid and the complex of the Moderna Museet and the
Arkitekturmuseet in Stockholm. A collection of rooms is the underlying concept
for the gallery spaces. The galleries may seem conventional, but their
organization within the building is guided by the desire for freedom. The
exhaustive studies undertaken to help design the skylights allow for optimum
lighting conditions combining natural and artificial light. Climate, light, circulation
through the space, dialogue between building and art, and simplicity and
elegance of materials are once again concerts that Moneo has addressed
thoughtfully and successfully in the new Beck Building.

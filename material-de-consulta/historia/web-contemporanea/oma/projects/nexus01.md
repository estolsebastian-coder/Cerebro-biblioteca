Project: Nexus Housing
Status: Completed March 1991
Client: Fukuoka Jisho Co.Ltd.
Budget: 12m euro
Location: Kashi District, Fukuoka, Japan
Site: Masterplan by Arata Isozaki: 2 plots of 1.791m2 and
1.706m2 in front of two future apartment towers by Isozaki
Program: 24 apartments, 4 shops, parking
Role: Architect for design and construction with local associated
architect
Built Area: 3.315m2 and 3.144m2

Partner in charge: Rem Koolhaas
Project Architect/Manager: Fuminori Hoshino
Team: Ron Steiner, Maartje Lammers, Marc Peeters, Xaveer de
Geyter, Ramon Klein, Leo v. Immerzeel, Jaap v. Heest, Shin-ichi
Kanefuji
Associated Architect: Yoshikazu Kawamura

Strcutural System: Reinforced concrete and steel
Major Materials: Black concrete wall in textured casting, zinc
alloy roof, aluminium windows with various kinds of glass
Interior Consultants: Kyoko Ohashi, Petra Blaisse
General Contractor and Structural Consultants: Maeda Corp

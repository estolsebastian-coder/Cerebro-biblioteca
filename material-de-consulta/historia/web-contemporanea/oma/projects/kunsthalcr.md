Project: Kunsthal
Status: Completed October 1992
Client: City of Rotterdam (Wim van Krimpen, first director of
Kunsthal)
Budget: 15m euro
Location: Rotterdam, The Netherlands
Site: 60x60 meter area on dike between major urban
\`highway` and southern edge of the Museumpark by OMA
(location of Boymans van Beuningen Museum and Netherlands
Architecture Institute). Adjacent to Nature Museum. Crossed by
a secondary road and a pedestrian ramp.
Program: Expo hall to display travelling exhibitions with three
major exhibition spaces, auditorium, and independently
accessible restaurant
Built Area: 7.000 m2
Structure: Concrete and steel

Lead Designer: Rem Koolhaas
Project Architect: Fuminori Hoshino
Team: Tony Adam, Isaac Batenburg, Leo van Immerzeel,
Herman Jacobs, Ron Steiner, Jeroen Thomas

Interior Consultants: Petra Blaisse, Kyoko Ohashi, Hans

The work of Rem Koolhaas and the OMA has won several
international awards, including the Pritzker Architecture Prize
(2000) and the Royal Institute of Bitish Architects Gold Medal
(2004).

In 2005 OMA received the European Union Prize for
Contemporary Architecture (Mies van der Rohe Prize) for the
Netherlands Embassy, Berlin; the American Institute of
Architects Honor Award for the Seattle Central Library, the
Dutch Design Prize for design of public space for the
Souterrain Underground Tram Tunnel and the Seoul
Metropolitan Government Grand Prize for Samsung Museum of
Art.

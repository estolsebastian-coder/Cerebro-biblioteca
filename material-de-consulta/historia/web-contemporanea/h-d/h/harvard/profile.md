Jacques Herzog
Design Critic
Department of Architecture

Profile

Jacques Herzog is Arthur Rotch Design Critic in Architecture. Pierre de Meuron is Arthur Rotch
Design Critic in Architecture. In 1978 de Meuron and Jacques Herzog established their office
as Jacques Herzog & Pierre de Meuron Partnership which became Herzog & de Meuron in
\1997. Harry Gugger and Christine Binswanger joined the office as Partners in 1991 and 1994
respectively, followed by Robert Hösl and Ascan Mergenthaler in 2004. Today the firm has 10
associates and about 180 employees worldwide, with branch offices in London, Munich, San
Francisco, Barcelona and Beijing.

In 2001 Jacques Herzog and Pierre de Meuron were awarded The Pritzker Architecture Prize
for their complete works. In October 2003 Herzog & de Meuron were awarded the RIBA
Stirling Prize for the Laban Dance Centre in London (Completion 2003).

Herzog & de Meuron received international attention very early in their career with the Blue
House (completed 1980) in Oberwil, Switzerland; the Stone House in Tavole, Italy (1988);
and the Apartment Building along a Party Wall in Basel (1988). The firm’s breakthrough
project was the Ricola Storage Building in Laufen, Switzerland (1987). The Goetz Collection, a
gallery for a private collection of modern art in Munich (1992) stands at the beginning of a
series of internationally acclaimed museum buildings such as the Küppersmühle Museum, for
the Grothe Collection in Duisburg, Germany (1999) and Tate Modern in London (2000).
Schaulager Laurenz Foundation, Basel (Completion 2003) creates a new type of space for art,
a warehouse for open storage of contemporary art. Renown in the United States came with
the Dominus Winery in Yountville, California (1998). In many projects the architects have
worked together with artists, an eminent example of that practice being the collaboration with
Remy Zaugg (Fünf Höfe, Five Courtyards for the Munich City Centre, 2003) and with Thomas
Ruff (for the Eberswalde Technical School Library in Germany, 1999).

Recently completed projects include the Prada Aoyama Epicenter, Prada's new flagship store
in Tokyo (Completion 2003); the new Library IKMZ in Cottbus (January 2005); Walker Art
Center Expansion in Minneapolis (April 2005) and the Allianz Arena, the new Soccer Stadium
for Munich (May 2005).

Current projects include among others the de Young Museum in San Francisco (opening 15
October 2005); The CaixaForum-Madrid (Completion 2006), the new exhibition space for
Fondacion La Caixa in Madrid; and the New Link Quay in Santa Cruz de Tenerife (Realization
2005-2009), comprising a new way to link the city of Santa Cruz with the Marina. After
building the Soccer Stadium St. Jakob Park in Basel (EM 2008) and the Allianz Area Munich
(FIFA World Cup 2006) . Herzog & de Meuron are realizing the National Stadium Beijing, the
main stadium of the 2008 Olympic Games.

Work by Herzog & de Meuron has appeared in numerous exhibitions and publications. In 2004
Schaulager Basel presented Herzog & de Meuron. No. 250. An Exhibition. This exhibition is
currently on display at Tate Modern London (until 29 August 2005) and will be travelling the
Haus der Kunst Munich, May through July 2006.

Nivel de potencia sonora

__________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Lw = 10 log (W / W 0 )

__________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

Formula de Sabine
Tr = 0,16 .Vol / Ab o Tr = 0,16 . Vol / S . αm

Tr = tiempo de reverberación (s)
Vol = volumen del espacio (m3)
S = área total de los cerramientos de la sala
αm = coeficiente de absorción medio
Ab = absorción total del espacio (Sabines)

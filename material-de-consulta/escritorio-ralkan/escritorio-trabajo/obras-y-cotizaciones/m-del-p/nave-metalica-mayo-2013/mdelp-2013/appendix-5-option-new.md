1 GENERAL
The delivery will include CHEMICAL STORAGE STEEL AND CONRETE
STRUCTURE WORKS in accordance with the following basic design data and
specification.

There are two different options and one of them shall be executed. First option is
completely new chemical storage. Second option is “Berkes” option, where shall be
used existing Berkes storages and built one new smaller storage compared to option
one. Option two quotation shall include only new building.

The Contract shall fulfil the requirements set forth in these documents.

The work will include all concrete and steel structures and the underground piping and
conduits as well as other embedded structures closely related to, or crucial for the
continuance of the concrete work.

Building structures will include:

Necessary excavation and backfilling
Underground infrastructures including drainage, potable water and sewage
Earthing and underground electrical cables to building and office room
Building foundations and ground floor
Delivery and installation of steel frame, roof structures and bracing
Delivery and installation of secondary supports for exterior walls and doors
Delivery and installation of wall cladding
Delivery and installation of roof cladding
Delivery and installation of finishing works of cladding including necessary
flashings, sealing and gutters with downpipes
Painting and finishing work for steel structures
Furnishing and installation of office room
Designing work for all structures including infrastructures
Electrical works above ground level are excluded.

2 LAWS AND REGULATIONS
All quoted material shall be in accordance with standards and regulations in force in
Uruguay.

The design will be made following the Eurocode 3.

3 BASIC DESIGN DATA
All design work including workshop design for primary and secondary steel structures
is in the contractor’s scope.

Detailed design of wall and roof cladding is part of the contractor’s scope.

Detailed design of cast in situ concrete structures is in contractor scope.

The design of the wall and roof cladding must be done using instructions and loadings
by FM Global.

For all constructions/buildings, to calculate outward/inward pressures at roofing and
cladding elements, it shall be employed FM Global Data Sheet DS 1-28 specification,
adopting surface roughness exposure D and basic wind velocity 50 m/s.

4 TECHNICAL INSTRUCTIONS
Technical instructions are described in separate specification appendixes to this paper.

5 SCOPE OF SUPPLY
The scope includes delivery and installation of primary steel structures such as roof
trusses, columns, beams and stiffeners as well as delivery and installation of
secondary steel structures. Also the roof outlets, gutters and doors are part of the
scope.

The installation and delivery of wall cladding, roofing and secondary support
structures are included in the scope.

The detailed design of steel and wall cladding is part of the contractor’s scope
including all cover strips.

Columns base bolts and grouting are part of contractor’s scope.

Underground pipes, earthing bars, cable conducts are in contractor’s scope.

The work also includes finishing work such as touch-up painting.

All cast in situ concrete works and included with design in contractor’s scope.

Pricing of the works:

Lump sum for executing and designing whole building from two options; option 1 “
new” and option 2 “Berkes”.

The end result of the work is a finished storage building fulfilling requirements set
forth in tender documentation including specifications and lay-out drawings.

1 CONSTRUCTION PROJECT

1.1 Description of Construction Object
The new pulp mill of Celulosa y Energía Punta Pereira S.A site is located in Montes
del Plata, Uruguay. This document deals with the Steel structures in the BOP-
package, regarding turbo generator, pipe bridge, cooling tower, water intake as well as
other auxiliary structures where the main detail design is made by Pöyry Finland.

1.2 Design and Execution of the Object
Pöyry Finland Oy is the responsible Designer of the steel and concrete platform
structures. Pöyry Oy prepares the necessary design documentation of the steel
structures both for the inquiry and also for the manufacturing.
Pöyry Finland Oy is the basic Designer of the claddings and the concrete wall and roof
structures of the objects. Detailed design of cladding is part of the suppliers scope.

1.3 Definition of the Execution Specification
The manufacturing and erection of the steel structures shall be carried out according to
this specification.

4.6 Tolerances of Structures
Steel structures shall be manufactured following the functional tolerances defined in
standard EN 1090-2 [1], Appendix D.2. The allowable tolerances deviating from the
tolerances defined in Appendix D.2 of standard EN 1090-2 [1] have been listed in
Appendices 1 and 2 of this document. Deviating requirements shall always be
indicated also on shop and erection drawings.

Definition:

Essential tolerances: Definition is valid for mechanical strength and stability of the
installed structure. These tolerances are only used in the strength calculations to define
those deviations, which have effect on the strength of the structure.

Functional tolerances: Definition is valid for compatibility, mountability and
appearance of the installed structure. These tolerances are used to define the permitted
tolerances, which are required during the manufacturing and erection.

According to EN 1090-2 both tolerance requirements are mandatory.

− Rotation of outer corner of bolt group ± 5 mm
− Center to center distance of two adjacent bolt groups ± 5 mm
− Center to center distance of two adjacent bolt groups in column line ± 5 mm
− Center to center distance of two adjacent bolt groups in girder line ± 5 mm
− Top level of bolt ± 10
mm
− Overall inclination of a bolt (L=length of bolt) ± L/100

Permitted imperfections of welds depending on welding class have been defined in

frequencies and methods are followed both in workshop manufacturing and in site
weldings.

\1. Visual inspection

Visually observed surface faults in execution classes EXC2, EXC3 and EXC4,
which exceed permitted limits of welding class, shall be further inspected by
liquid penetrantion testing or magnetic particle testing.
\2. NDT testing

welds. Extent of testing is in accordance with Table 24 of standard EN 1090-2
[1]. Testing is carried out to structures and welds according to attached Tables 12
and 13.
For full penetration V and K welds the testing method is ultrasonic testing.

\3. Reporting

require corrective action shall be reported by nonconformance report (NCR) and
correction plan.
\4. Corrections to be made
Corrective welding work of a defective weld shall be carried out in accordance
with approved Welding Procedure Specification (WPS).

− Hidden toe area (as Case B) shall always be welded.

deviations may be permitted for the alignment between the root edges or root
faces of in-line butt joints between hollow section components:

− 25 % of the thickness of the thinner constituent product for material ≤ 12
mm thick
− 3 mm for material over 12 mm thick.

This alignment may be achieved using machining of ends to correct wall
thickness variations and ovality or out-of-squareness of hollow sections,
provided that the remaining material thickness complies with the minimum
specified.

For in-line splice butt joints between hollow sections of different thickness, the

− if the difference in thickness does not exceed 1,5 mm, no special measures
are necessary
− if the difference in thickness does not exceed 3 mm, the backing material
may be shaped to accommodate the difference (local hot forming of the
backing material may be used)
− if the difference exceeds 3 mm the wall of the thicker component should

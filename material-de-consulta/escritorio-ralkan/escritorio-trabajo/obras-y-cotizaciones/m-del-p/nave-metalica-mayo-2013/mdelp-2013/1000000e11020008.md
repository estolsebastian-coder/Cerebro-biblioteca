1 GENERAL
Earthing system shall be constructed in compliance with valid IEC standards, local
laws and authorities’ demanding.

2 EARTHING SYSTEM
Earthing system shall fulfil the condition of the global earthing system specifications
(IEC 61936-1). Global earthing system consists of:

\- earthing electrodes and earthing conductors
\- earthing/equipotential bars
\- protective conductors and protective bonding conductors
\- lightning protective conductors and lightning ground electrodes

All these items together will ensure that there are no dangerous touch voltages.

3 EARTHING ELECTRODE
Earthing electrode will be made by using 70 mm² bare stranded copper conductors
which will be laid in the ground around and cross the buildings foundation.

The earthing electrode shall be pulled-up above floor level at least in every
transformer rooms, in main electrical room/cable room and convenient intervals of the
building column (approximately every 3rd column around building). In these places
earthing electrode will be connected to the main equipotential bonding/earthing bars.

Reinforcement steels of foundation will be connected to the earthing electrode at least
at the same column where the electrode has been taken up above the floor level.
Connection will be made under ground by using rounding steel bar (10 mm stainless
steel) which will be welded to reinforcement steels and connected to the earthing
electrode.

It is recommended that the earthing electrodes of the different buildings will be
connected together by bare 70 mm² Cu conductor laid in ground.

All underground connections to the earthing electrode will be made by using thermo-
welding (Cadweld) connection.

4 EQUIPOTENTIAL BONDING

4.1 Equipotential bonding bars
The bare copper main equipotential bonding bars (EB-bars), shall be installed in each
place where the building earthing electrode has been taken up above the floor level,
e.g. in every transformer room, electrical room or associated cable room. Earthing
electrode will be connected to these bars. Additionally more bars shall be installed in
the process area, close to equipment that need equipotential bonding.

All exposed- and extraneous conductive parts in buildings which have to be earthed
shall be connected to these bars. The size of each bar is depending on a number of
connected conductors. See appendix IV.

4.2 Equipotential bonding network
The equipotential bonding network consists of EB-bars which will be interconnected
by using main equipotential bonding conductors (120 mm² Cu yegn). These bonding
conductors will be installed in grid form on main cable routes in each building.

Every building has it´s own equipotential network and they all will be connected
together by main equipotential bonding conductors (120 mm² Cu yegn). These
bonding conductors will be installed on in underground way between the buildings.

4.3 Equipotential bonding conductors
All simultaneously accessible exposed conductive parts and extraneous conductive
parts (where needed) shall be earthed with equipotential bonding conductors direct to
the nearest EB-bar of the main equipotential-network. Typical items which are
connected to equipotential network are:

\- PE-bars of SWG’s and MCCs
\- metal parts of electrical rooms
\- cable trays
\- pipes and tanks
\- metal structures
\- exposed conductive parts

4.3.1 Cable trays
Each tray is travel by a ground wire 70 mm² connected every 1,5 meters with cooper
presses, and will be connected at intervals every 50 meters to the bar bonding. If there
is way of two or more levels of tray, only the upper ladder will be travelled by earth
cable 70 mm², but each strip tray level will be connected to the cable below the first
level at least every 3-6 meters depending of delivered section lengths.

4.3.2 Cranes
Crane rails will be connected to the equipotential bonding network. See appendix V.

4.3.3 Pipes
All incoming conductive pipes inside the buildings will be earthed to the nearest EB-
bar. Connections will be made by 70 mm² Cu conductor. Additionally the pipes have
to be earthed on the pipe bridge. See appendixes VII and VIII.

4.3.4 Motors
PE-wire of the every motor cable shall be connected to PE-bar at MCC and to PE-
terminal at terminal box of the motor. See appendixes I, II and III.

LV-motors frame (over 90 kW) shall be connected to the nearest EB-bars (if needed).

4.3.5 Major Process Equipment
Major process equipment and large metal constructions will be earthed to the nearest
EB-bar by using 70 mm² Cu conductors. Preferably the connection will be made by
connecting equipment to at least two different bars.

4.4 Supplementary eguipotential bonding
Supplementary bonding will be done when necessary according to standards (IEC
60364-5-54).

5 SYSTEM EARTHING
The power distribution systems and earthing methods which will be used are:

System Earthing method

150 kV Solidly earthed

33 kV Earthed with resistor 4x100A

6,6 kV IT system, Earthed with resistor. Earth-fault current is
10A. See appendix I.

690 V IT system, earthed with resistor. Earth-fault current is
0,8A. System earthing will be done from the main
switchgear PE-bar to main equipotential bar/earth by
2×120 Cu conductor. See appendix II.

400 V Solidly earthed TN-S system. System earthing will be done
from the main switchgear PE-bar to main equipotential
bar/earth by 2×120 Cu conductor. See appendix III.

6 EQUIPMENT EARTHING

6.1 General
Equipment earthing will be made with earthing conductors which are required to run
with (or close by) the circuit conductors to provide a permanent, low-impedance
conductive path for ground-fault current.

6.2 Transformers
Transformer high voltage sides earthing will be made by connecting HV-cable sheaths
and transformer tank to EB-bar in the transformer rooms. See appendix I, II and III.

In low voltage side, transformer tank will be connected to the switchgear/MCC
earthing bar (PE). Connection provides a low-impedance path for the fault current.
See appendix I, II and III.

6.3 Power Switchgear
MV-switchgears (33 kV and 6,6 kV) shall have earthed PE-bars. All MV-cable
sheaths will be connected to these bars.

690 V main switchgears have earthed PE-bars. PE-conductors of installation cables
will be connected to the PE-bars of the switchgears.

Sub MCCs (690 V) PE-bar will be earthed through the ground conductor (PE) of the
MCC feeder cable.

6.4 Lighting Boards and Panels
Each lighting distribution main board (400 V) has earthed PE-bar which will be
connected to the feeding transformer neutral pole. The separation of N-PE is done
inside the board. All cables shall have 5-conductors (TN-S) and cables PE-conductors
will be connected to the PE-bar of board.

Each lighting sub panel PE-bar shall be earthed through the protective conductors
(PE) of feeder cable.

6.5 Motors
The metallic sheaths/armour of MV-motor feeder cable(s) shall be connected to the
motor frame and another end to the PE-bar of the feeding SWG. Additionally motor
frame shall be connected to the nearest equipotential bar by 70 mm² Cu connector
(equipotential bonding).

Low voltage motors shall be earthed through the motor feeder cable(s) protective
conductor. The frame of large motors (over 90 kW) shall be connected to the nearest
equipotential bar (if needed). Size of this wire shall be a half of the size of the phase
core of the motor feeder cable but not less than 50 mm² Cu.

6.6 Power Cables
Metallic sheaths (metal enclosures, armours, screens) of the power cables with
nominal voltages above 1 kV should be earthed at both ends.

7 FE-NETWORKS
Separate FE-earthing network is not used. FE will be connected to the PE-earth bar
inside the rack. See appendix III.

8 EXPLOSION HAZARD AREA
All metal tanks, conductive pipes and PE-terminals of electrical equipment shall be
connected to the equipotential bonding bar. Equipotential bondings inside the
explosion hazard area shall be done according IEC-60079-14 standard.

9 LIGHTNING PROTECTION
All buildings with explosion hazard area, tanks containing flammable liquid and high
chimneys shall be protected according IEC 61024-1-2 standard.

10 EARTHING BAR NUMBERING
Earthing bars are to be numbered by the nearest main equipment such as Transformer,
MCC or tank. At the process area some bars shall be numbered by column numbers.
Last two characters are always EB.

For example:

At the bay of transformer +130TE01 earthing bar is +130TE01EB.

For MCC +130AE01 earthing bar is +130AE01EB.

Near Tank +147T101 earthing bar is +147T101EB.

On column J/6 at Drying Machine 1 earthing bar is +130J/6EB.

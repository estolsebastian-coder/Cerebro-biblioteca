The aim of this paper is to present a new methodological approach for the study of megalithic
buildings in the Levant by presenting the case study of the megalithic monument ST_004 from
the cemetery of Qana Maabour in Menjez (Akkar, Lebanon). Conducted within the framework of
the MEG-A Project — First Megalith in the Northern Levant (2022–2026) — led by T. SteimerHerbet and Z. Wygnan´ska, this research aims to characterize the cultural and technical
dynamics that inspired the construction of these monuments. We seek to explore the megalithic
building techniques used by ancient builders, reconstruct the history of these monuments
through the application of new tools in the field of buildings archaeology, and uncover the
symbolic practices that guided their design through a geoarchaeological approach.

Keywords Buildings archaeology, geoarchaeology, megalithic tombs, Late Chalcolithic, Early Bronze Age, Lebanon, Akkar
